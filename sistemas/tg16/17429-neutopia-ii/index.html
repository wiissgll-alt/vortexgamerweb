Neutopia II

Descripción

Some years have passed since the brave hero jazeta destroyed dirth, the emperor of darkness. Peace has returned to the land of neutopia. But there are still monsters roaming the countryside, and some of them were spotted near yurius shrine. Jazeta went there to find out what exactly had happened. But he didn't return... You are jazeta's son, and you must first of all find your missing father! can it be that the shadow of emperor of darkness threatens the peaceful land again?.. The sequel to "neutopia" is similar to the predecessor in every way. Navigating your hero from a top-down view, you explore the vast countryside, fighting monsters in real time combat. The game is not a true rpg, but an action adventure: you don't gain any experience points from combat, only collect money to buy supplies. In dungeons, you must use bombs on walls to reveal secret passages, push stones, and fight many regular enemies and bosses.

Mais il y a encore des monstres qui rôdent dans la campagne, et certains d'entre eux ont été repérés près du sanctuaire de Yurius. Jazeta s'y rendit pour découvrir ce qui s'était exactement passé. Mais il n'est pas revenu... La suite de "Neutopia" est similaire à son prédécesseur en tous points. En pilotant votre héros depuis une vue de haut en bas, vous explorez la vaste campagne et combattez des monstres en temps réel. Le jeu n'est pas un véritable RPG, mais une action-aventure : vous ne gagnez aucun point d'expérience en combat, vous collectez uniquement de l'argent pour acheter des fournitures. Dans les donjons, vous devez utiliser des bombes sur les murs pour révéler des passages secrets, pousser des pierres et combattre de nombreux ennemis et boss réguliers.

Il seguito di "Nutopia" è simile in tutto al predecessore. Navigando con il tuo eroe da una visuale dall'alto verso il basso, esplori la vasta campagna, combattendo mostri in combattimenti in tempo reale. Il gioco non è un vero gioco di ruolo, ma un'avventura d'azione: non guadagni punti esperienza dal combattimento, raccogli solo denaro per acquistare rifornimenti. Nei sotterranei, devi usare le bombe sui muri per rivelare passaggi segreti, spingere pietre e combattere molti nemici e boss regolari.

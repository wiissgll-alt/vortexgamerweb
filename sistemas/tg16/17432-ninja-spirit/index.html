Ninja Spirit

Descripción

Conversion of a ninja-based action game that has the player leading moonlight through numerous enemy-riddled levels on a quest to exact revenge against his father's murderer. To accomplish this task moonlight is armed with a katana, shurikens, bombs, and a sickle and chain weapon. Each weapon offers its own advantages and must be used at certain times to overcome specific obstacles or opponents. The game's coin-op origins are evident in the fact that it does not have passwords or saves. To compensate for this, however, adjustable difficulty settings were added.

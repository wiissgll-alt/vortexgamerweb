Tricky Kick

Descripción

Tricky kick is a sliding block puzzle game for the turbografix-16. You have to kick two of the same item together, and they disappear, until you clear the board of pieces. You can bump one item with another unless the way is blocked. There are also enemies that you have to kick into bumpers to destroy them. The game has a regular mode and a special mode with 60 puzzles each. Many of the puzzles are quite difficult even in regular mode.

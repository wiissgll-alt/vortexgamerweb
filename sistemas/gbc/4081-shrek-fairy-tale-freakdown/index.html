Shrek: Fairy Tale Freakdown

Descripción

In this street fighter 2-style 2d one-on-one beat 'em up, you can choose to play as characters from the movie shrek, including shrek himself, the gingerbread man, thelonius, and six other included personalities. All characters have their own signature moves, but as you move on through the quest more special powers are unlocked - these include speed, invincibility, and strength. Shrek: fairy tale freakdown appears to be aimed to towards young children, due to the character theme and the lack of blood. It is purely a single player game.

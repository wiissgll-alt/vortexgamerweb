International Karate 2000

Descripción

International karate 2000 is a remake of the classic beat 'em up international karate in which you play as a new entry in the international karate championship and travel the world in hopes of defeating each local champion in tournament-legal (for points) matches. The game is played from a non-scrolling side view, with your attacks being determined by combinations of the attack buttons and the directional arrows. As mentioned you win matches via points awarded whenever you connect a direct hit or perform a successful move. The gbc version also adds a health bar that influences your performance and a round-based system. Furthermore all graphics have been redrawn and the character selection has been expanded and now includes 12 playable characters, each from a different location.

Diva Starz: Mall Mania

Descripción

Join the divas alexa, nikki, tia and summer in this portable collection of 6 mini games, on their way to the fashion show. The mini games include: "the scooter spot", which is a simple ramp hitting/obstacle avoiding cruise to the mall. "the fashion spot", a conveyor belt sorting game, where you toss items in to boxes. "the music spot", a simplified dance dance revolution beat hitting game. "the snack spot", a tower of hanoi meets an ice cream sundae. "the pet shop", where you catch a pet in a simple maze. And finally, "the style spot", where you dress your diva and match sets of clothes.

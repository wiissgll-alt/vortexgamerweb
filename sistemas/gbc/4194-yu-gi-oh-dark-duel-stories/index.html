Descripción

Dark duel stories brings the yu-gi-oh! card game to you via gameboy color. Battle your way past opponents from the tv show and manga as well as your friends using the link cable. In dark duel stories, you must defeat your opponents to advance and eventually make it to the four rulers of ancient egypt who have the millennium pieces. As you win battles, you will get new cards as well as card parts that can be used to construct more cards. Set up your deck with monsters, traps, and spells to help you defeat your opponents. And don't forget to bring along some fusion monsters to help when you get into trouble, or just to help finish off your opponent.

Descripción

This game is the sequel to nba showtime. The gameplay has been improved quite a bit. Now you play 3-on-3 instead of 2-on-2. There are also a few new moves added such as alley-oops. The gameplay is still very similar to last year's game but the added extras are welcome and makes the game a little more enjoyable to play.

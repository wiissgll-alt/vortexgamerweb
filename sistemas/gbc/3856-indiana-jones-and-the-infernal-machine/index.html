Indiana Jones and the Infernal Machine

Descripción

Whereas the other versions of indiana jones' first game in nearly a decade are 3d action adventures inspired by tomb raider, this edition is different, being viewed from above in pseudo-3d with multi-directional movement. The plot involves communists attempting to rebuild an ancient time machine, whose pieces are spread across 19 levels. There are largely-switch-based puzzles to solve on each one, and different routes through to completing them. Indy is variously armed with a pistol, machine gun, grenades and his ubiquitous whip. Each level has a password, and ends with a clip advancing the story. As well as soviets, your foes include scorpions, snakes and jellyfish.

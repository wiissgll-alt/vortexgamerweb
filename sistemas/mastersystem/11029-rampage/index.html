Descripción

Rampage players take control of gigantic monsters trying to survive against onslaughts of military forces. Each round is completed when a particular city is completely reduced to rubble. Up to three simultaneous players control the monsters george (a king kong-like gorilla), lizzie (a godzilla-like dinosaur/lizard), or ralph (a giant werewolf), created from mutated humans.

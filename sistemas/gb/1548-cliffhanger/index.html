Descripción

Based on the film of the same name. A plane filled with terrorists attempting to steal money from a treasury plane while airborne is shot down by an fbi plane. The terrorists survive and send out a distress signal, which the main character, gabe, responds to. However, gabe does not know that the mayday signal is coming from a group of terrorists, and after reaching them, the terrorists capture gabe's partner, hal, and hold him hostage. Gabe must then set out and retrieve the money in order to save hal.

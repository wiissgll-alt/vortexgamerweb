Descripción

Castlevania: the adventure, known as dracula densetsu (ドラキュラ伝説, officially translated the legend of dracula) in japan, is a platform game released for the game boy in 1989. It is the first castlevania title for the system. Set a century before the events of the original castlevania, the player controls an ancestor of simon belmont named christopher belmont who goes on a quest to defeat dracula.

Descripción

Target earth is about earth's outcasts, who have become cyborgs, returning from space to attack the civilization that remained behind. The earth defense league fights to defend earth in a galactic war. The battle begins on ganymede. The game alternates between battles in space, on planets, on the earth and inside enemy outposts.

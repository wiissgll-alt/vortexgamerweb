Kyuukai Douchuuki

Descripción

Kyuukai douchuuki (球界道中記) is a 1990 arcade baseball game by namco which they ported to the sega mega drive in 1991. Neither version left japan. The game is a generic baseball game where you are only in control of the pitcher and batter; once the batter hits the ball, you can only watch what your team does. When batting, the d-pad moves around and c swings. When pitching, left and right move around and some amount of time holding the c button appears to pitch.

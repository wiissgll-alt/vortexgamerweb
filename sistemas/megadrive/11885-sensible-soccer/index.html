Descripción

Sensible soccer, or sensible soccer: european champions is an entry in the sensible soccer series of football games. Despite its name, the sega versions of sensible soccer are in fact based upon the amiga/atari st sensible soccer 92/93, an update to the "real" sensible soccer which was released exclusively for these computers. This sensible soccer was released for the sega mega drive, sega mega-cd, sega master system and sega game gear. In north america, the mega cd version is called championship soccer '94. An update in the form of sensible soccer: international edition was made available for the mega drive.

Descripción

This is a hack of ultimate mortal kombat 3 for the sega genesis. It adds nearly all legit characters from mortal kombat 1, 2, and trilogy, and re-adds the missing sheeva. The only character not included is the mk3 version of noob saibot. It also adds backgrounds, new moves, combos, finishing moves, and missing audio samples are restored. It’s the ultimate mortal kombat hack.

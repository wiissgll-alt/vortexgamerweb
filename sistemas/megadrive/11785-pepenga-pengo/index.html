Pepenga pengo (ぺぺんがｐｅｎｇｏ), sometimes just known as pengo, is a sequel to the 1982 arcade game, pengo. It was released for the sega mega drive exclusively in japan in 1995, and was the last first-party sega mega drive game to be released in that region.

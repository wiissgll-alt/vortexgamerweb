Descripción

Pyramid magic ii (ピラミッドマジックii) is a puzzle-platform game for the sega mega drive, released exclusively in japan via the sega game toshokan service. Pyramid magic ii claims to be a "sequel" to pyramid magic, though is more of a continuation of the original game, with identical graphics, sounds and gameplay.

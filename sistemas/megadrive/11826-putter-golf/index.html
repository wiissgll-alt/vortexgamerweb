Putter Golf

Descripción

Putter golf (パターゴルフ) is a golf game developed and published by sega for the sega mega drive. It was only available in japan as a download through the sega game toshokan service. Putter golf is essentially (but whether or not it officially is is unknown) a mega drive version of putt & putter: a very simple golf game with an isometric perspective. Rather than compete on wide open courts however, the game opts for much smaller levels which would only require a putter (much like minigolf). As well as this, there are a number of obstacles which can get in your way, from bumpers to lava.

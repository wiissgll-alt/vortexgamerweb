Sega Game Gear

5 in 1 Funpak

Descripción

5 in one fun pak is a collection of well-known games for the sega game gear. Included are versions of chess, checkers, backgammon, reversi and yacht (using yahtzee's rules), and the player can choose whether to play against the computer or another human. Each game (except yacht) has difficulty settings. The game was released exclusively in north america in 1994.

Super Smash Bros.

Descripción

Super smash brothers takes familiar nintendo characters such as mario, kirby and donkey kong, and puts them into battle! there are 8 fully-interactive landscapes, with one for each character and noticeable links to their games. Controls are analogue-based, with different moves executed depending on the direction and how firmly the stick is pressed. Items like baseball bats, bob-ombs, and healing items drop into the arenas at random, aiding any nearby characters than can make use of them. The game uses a fixed viewpoint which scrolls, although characters are rendered in 3d. The one-player mode has 14 stages to work through. Up to 4 human players can do battle at once, with team play as well as all-against-all action. Hidden characters are thrown in for good measure, as are bonus rounds, and a battle to the end with a giant hand (yeah, you read right!). Playable characters include: captain falcon* (f-zero), donkey kong, fox (star fox), kirby, jigglypuff* (pokémon), link (legend of zelda), luigi* (mario), mario, ness* (mother/earthbound), pikachu (pokémon), samus (metroid) and yoshi (mario) *character must be unlocked first

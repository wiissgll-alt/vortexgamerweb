Descripción

Wcw/nwo revenge is the much anticipated sequel to thq's smash "wcw vs nwo: world tour". With greatly improved graphics, animation, roster, control and much anticipated 40 man 'battle royal', revenge is a definite step up over it's predecessor. Take control of one of 50 wcw wrestlers (and 10+ fictional wrestlers) such as sting or hollywood hogan and battle through for any of the 5 different title belts with full statistical tracking of wins/losses/draws and new replay mode you can gloat in your victory or powerful move over and over again.

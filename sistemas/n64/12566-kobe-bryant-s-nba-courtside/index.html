Kobe Bryant's NBA Courtside

Descripción

Fully endorsed and featuring motion captured moves of the man himself, kobe bryant, nba courtside has all the teams and players from the 1998 nba season. Play modes include the usual 82 game regular season, playoffs, exhibition and the ability to create your own player in the create-a-player mode. The players themselves, using the motion captured moves of kobe, use a range of different basketball moves for the full-on experience, including many different dunk, dribbling and passing techniques, as well as a high range of artificial intelligence.

Pienamente approvato e caratterizzato dai movimenti catturati dall'uomo stesso, Kobe Bryant, NBA Courtside contiene tutte le squadre e i giocatori della stagione NBA 1998. Consejos

  • Usa el poste bajo con jugadores altos; es la forma más fácil de anotar cerca del aro.
  • Use the low post with tall players; It's the easiest way to score near the rim.
  • Utilisez le poste bas avec les joueurs de grande taille ; C'est le moyen le plus simple de marquer près du bord.
  • Usa il post basso con giocatori alti; È il modo più semplice per segnare vicino al ferro.

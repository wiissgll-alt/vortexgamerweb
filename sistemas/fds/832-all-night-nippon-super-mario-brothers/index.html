Descripción

All night nippon - super mario bros. Was a promotional item given out as a contest prize by the japanese radio show "all night nippon" to celebrate its twentieth year on the air. It combines levels from both super mario bros 1 & 2 and also changes graphics of items and their placements as well as graphics of enemies and mushroom retainers to members of the show.

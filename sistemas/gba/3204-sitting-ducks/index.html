Descripción

Welcome to the whimsical world of sitting ducks, where life is good, food's a plenty, and alligators are lurking in neighboring swampwood. One bird stands out amongst the flock: bill. He is a quirky, smaller than average duck with a big imagination and a secret fun loving side.

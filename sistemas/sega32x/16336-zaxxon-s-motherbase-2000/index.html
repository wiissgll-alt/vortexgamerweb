Descripción

This 32x-exclusive sci-fi shooter has gameplay is somewhat like sega arcade classic zaxxon. The graphics consist of a diagonally scrolling playfield which creates a pseudo 3d environment. The backgrounds are drawn using 2d scrolling planes, but the enemy and ship objects are drawn using 3d flat-shaded polygons. Although the basic goal of each of the 9 levels is to shoot everything without getting shot, the game has a strategic element. Your basic spacecraft has very little firepower, and can only sustain one hit. To make matters worse, there are very few power-ups. In order to survive, you have to jump on top of enemies and take control of their weapons systems. This system allows for a large variety of player-controllable ships.

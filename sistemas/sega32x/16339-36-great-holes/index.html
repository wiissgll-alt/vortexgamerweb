Descripción

As the title implies, in 36 great holes the player takes control of 24 different digitized golfers  12 males and 12 females  playing in 27 historic courses in the u.s. Including disney's eagle pines and tidewater golf club, playing 36 authentic holes. There are seven different modes (stroke play, tournament, skins, shoot-out, scramble, practice and match play) and three different types of golf club heads including wood, metal and graphite.

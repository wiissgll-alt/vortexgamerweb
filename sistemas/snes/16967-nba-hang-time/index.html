NBA Hang Time

Descripción

Nba hang time is a 2-on-2 arcade basketball action game following in the footsteps of nba jam. All nba teams, each featuring 4 players to choose from, are included along with fast paced, over-the-top basketball, disregarding the normal rules of the game but including fouls and out of bounds. You can also create your own player, with a collection of body parts and clothing to choose from.

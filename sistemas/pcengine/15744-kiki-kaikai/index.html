Kiki Kaikai

Descripción

An arcade game released by taito about a priestess named sayo-chan who must rescue the gods of fortune from monsters. Kiki kaikai ("mysterious ghost world") is a scrolling action/shooter featuring the shrine maiden sayo-chan as she fights a horde of youkai and oni in a quest to recover the gods of fortune. It is a precursor to the pocky & rocky games for the super nintendo. Sayo-chan/pocky makes her way across various levels, changing directions occasionally, and using either a wand or scrolls to exorcise the unruly spirits she meets. She can only get hit once before dying, though some enemies will grab her instead to slow her down.

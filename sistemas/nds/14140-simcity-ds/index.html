Simcity DS

Descripción

Simcity ds brings the classic series to the handheld consoles. As the mayor placed in charge of running their own city, players use the touchscreen to build up and run their town, providing commercial, residential and industrial zones, protection against crime and fire, entertainment areas and even airports, highways and railways for the simcitizens to travel. Players also have to contend with natural (and unnatural) disasters, the pressures of an ever-expanding city, and of course, public opinion, which can make a job very difficult if things get out of control. New to this version is the inclusion of wireless communication with other mayors via a post office. Players can send letters to each other, giving a short summary of the state of their city. When players send letters to each other, they earn special monuments for their cities. Additionally, when disaster strikes, the mayor can take a more hands-on approach in directly rescuing their simcitizens.

Simcity ds apporte la série classique aux consoles portables. En tant que maire chargé de gérer leur propre ville, les joueurs utilisent l'écran tactile pour construire et gérer leur ville, offrant des zones commerciales, résidentielles et industrielles, une protection contre la criminalité et les incendies, des zones de divertissement et même des aéroports, des autoroutes et des voies ferrées pour que les citoyens puissent voyager. Les joueurs doivent également faire face à des catastrophes naturelles (et non naturelles), aux pressions d'une ville en constante expansion et, bien sûr, à l'opinion publique, ce qui peut rendre la tâche très difficile si les choses deviennent incontrôlables. Inoltre, quando si verifica una catastrofe, il sindaco può adottare un approccio più attivo nel salvare direttamente i propri cittadini.

Break 'EM ALL

Descripción

This breakout clone for the nintendo ds offers touch screen controls for directing your bat. As well as a traditional "tokoton mode" offering 50 screens of bricks to destroy, it also offers over 3 million random levels to go for a high score. There is also "quest mode" where you fight through 12 levels and face 8 bosses. Finally "survival mode" where 8 bats attempt to knock balls at each other's weak point to defeat them. Power-ups are available in tokoton mode but you select which 4 you wish to use out of a total 8, including bat lengtheners and weapons. It also offers up to 8 player single or multi-card play locally for quest and survival mode.

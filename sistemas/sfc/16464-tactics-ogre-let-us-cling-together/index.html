Tactics ogre is a very detailed 3-d isometric turn based war / rpg sim. You have the option to recruit characters, control individual equipment of each character, with over 6 male character classes, 6 female character classes, 3 demi-human classes, and more. You can also train at safe locations to advance your army before the next battle / storyline advancement.

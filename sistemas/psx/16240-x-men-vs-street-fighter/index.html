X-men vs. Street fighter is a crossover fighting video game developed and published by capcom. As the title suggests, the game includes characters from marvel's x-men franchise and the cast from capcom's street fighter series. X-men vs. Street fighter features gameplay similar to street fighter, but incorporates dual-character selection and tag team-based combat. Each player selects two characters to compete in a one-on-one battle, attempting to defeat the opposing team. The players are given the ability to switch between their characters at any point during the match.

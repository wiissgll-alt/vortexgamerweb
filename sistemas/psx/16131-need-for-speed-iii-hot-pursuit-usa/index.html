Descripción

Need for speed returns with all the exotic supercars, tracks, and race modes you've come to expect--but reengineered for higher performance, stunning realism, and fierce competition. With hot pursuit mode, you can race on both sides of the law: as a cop using radar, dispatchers, and roadblocks to track down speeders; or as a racer, trying to outrun the law. Avec le mode poursuite, vous pouvez courir des deux côtés de la loi : en tant que flic utilisant des radars, des répartiteurs et des barrages routiers pour traquer les speeders ; ou en tant que coureur, essayant de contourner la loi. Consejos

  • Atajos de policía: En el modo Hot Pursuit, los policías pueden usar bloqueos. Aprende a frenar bruscamente y girar 90 grados para evitarlos si ves las luces en el horizonte.
  • Control de tracción: Si el coche se vuelve inestable en lluvia, suelta el acelerador en el ápice de la curva antes de salir acelerando.
  • Police Shortcuts: In Hot Pursuit mode, police officers can use blockades. Learn to brake sharply and turn 90 degrees to avoid them if you see the lights on the horizon.
  • Traction control: If the car becomes unstable in the rain, release the accelerator at the apex of the corner before accelerating away.

Battle hunter is an anime-styled tactical role-playing game, released for the playstation in 1999. The game revolves around a player-controlled hunter that must compete with three other hunters in order to win a relic, and makes heavy use of traditional rpg conventions such as dice and tile-based movement. In the years after a major world war, the few scientists remaining on earth set out to develop a formula to protect humanity from extinction. To be successful they require vital information from the ruins of cities around the world, and assign the task of collecting it to an elite task force known as the hunters. The story revolves around the player getting jobs from the shop owner to recover relics of interest from dungeons. Eventually the player will compete against a secret organization known as b phs for the target relics.

Darkstalkers, known in japan as vampire, is a series of 2d fighting games developed and published by capcom, beginning with darkstalkers: the night warriors (vampire: the night warriors) in 1994. The series is set in a pastiche gothic horror universe and features a highly stylized, cartoonish graphic style. It features characters based on either various kinds of monsters (the titular "darkstalkers") or their hunters, who are all engaging in combat against each other. Darkstalkers introduced many of the concepts used in later capcom fighting games, including its first use of 16-bit color animation sprites and gameplay mechanics later employed in the street fighter alpha and marvel vs. Capcom series.

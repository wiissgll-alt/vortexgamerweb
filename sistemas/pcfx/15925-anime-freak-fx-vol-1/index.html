Anime Freak FX: Vol. 1

Descripción

Nec had the idea of releasing what is essentially a pc-fx game magazine in a game cd format. This is the first of what will later become bi-annual anime freak releases. It contains: 1. A demo of akazukin cha-cha a slide show of photos of voice actresses a karaoke mode for some of the songs used in pc-fx titles a collection of movies dedicated to lilith, the heroine of megami paradise ii pc-fx-related news "voice idol" mode, which contains information about songs used in pc-fx games the first episode of an anime mini-series called "private eye doll" a "making of..." section. What still allows this cd to qualify as a game is the fact that it also contains a rock-paper-scissors mini-game, which is played against a real actress. The player has to press one of the three main pc-fx controller buttons in time, which correspond to the international hand gestures for rock, paper, and scissors.

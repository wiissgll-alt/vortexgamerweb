Anime Freak FX: Vol. 3

Descripción

The third volume of anime freak fx contains: 1. Continuations of the anime mini-series started in the first two volumes, "private eye doll" and "voice idol" 2. Several anime fmvs dedicated to ryou-ouki, the spaceship/funny animal from tenchi muyou: ryou-ouki 3. Karaoke mode 4. New in this volume: the voice actress junko iwao in a special video 5. "voice & talk", a slide show of photos of voice actresses, including a diary-like commentary 6. "making of" section 7. New: presentation of 20 prettiest girls from pc-fx games, one-by-one, starting with nr. 20 8. Demo of an upcoming neon genesis evangelion game, which never made it to the pc-fx 9. Trailer for angelique special 10. Information about anime-making and voice actors in particular pc-fx-related news, and finally 11. A game called heart o sagase, meaning "look for the heart". As before, the game is played against a real actress. The goal of the game is to find the heart belonging to a little penguin. The player is shown 10 boxes, only one of which contains the heart. The player and the actress take turns in guessing where the heart might be. Each turn has a time limit of 10 seconds. The box can either contain the heart, be empty, or have a "chance" icon inside, which will grant an additional turn.

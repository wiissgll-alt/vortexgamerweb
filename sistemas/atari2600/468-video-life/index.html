Descripción

Video life turns your tv into a doodle tablet on which you can create detailed pictures, patterns and symbols. Video life will then start your picture pulsating and growing into new and unexpected patterns, with musical tone accompaniment. Your imagination combined with the game computer's tireless development of any pattern are an ideal creative combination. The endless variations possible ensure that there will always be new and surprising patterns to discover.

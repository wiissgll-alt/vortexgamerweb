Super Trio

Descripción

A platform / run jump scrolling game. You controls one of the three animals characters, a mouse named jake a cat named tomi and a dog named brudo each of one equipped with different weapons, through varied and colorful levels, you must jump, climbing and pick up items while you defend or dodge all the enemies in the way, to reach the exit to face off the enemy boss and go to bonus stage or next level.

To gain their powers which the 'stones' hold for the promised victory, many nations fought over the mystic stones and reheated the world of war. Within all the 'stones' is the 'medallion of wisdom', known as the power source of all mystic stones, it had enough energy to terminate humanity. The whole world was in despair.

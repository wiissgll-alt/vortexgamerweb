Tondemo Crisis (Japan)

Descripción

A bizarre game, featuring funny mini-games the game follows each member of a japanese, working-class family of four members on their bizarre adventures home from their daily routines to get birthday presents for the family's grandmother haruko. The game consists of several action-oriented minigames strung together and book-ended with pre-rendered cutscenes. The game was designed and scripted by kenichi nishi and features music from the tokyo ska paradise orchestra.

Descripción

A vertical scrolling shooter. Pilot your spaceship through the deep space, collect various speed and weapon items to take down a multitude of enemies, and restore peace to the galaxy! the game concept is all about the power-ups. The game offers ten items to pick up, including speed boosts, laser weapons, a spread gun, a flamethrower, a tiny spaceship helpers and missiles.

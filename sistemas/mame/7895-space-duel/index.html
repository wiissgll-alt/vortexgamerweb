Space Duel

Descripción

1,895 space duel is the first and only multi-player interactive vector game by atari. When "asteroids deluxe" tanked, this game was taken off the shelf and released to moderate success. A space duel cabinet appears on the cover of the who's 1982 album, 'it's hard'. In 1982, atari released a set of 12 collector pins including : "missile command", "battle zone", "tempest", "asteroids deluxe", "space duel", "centipede", "gravitar", "dig dug", "kangaroo", "xevious", "millipede" and "food fight". - tips and tricks - * invite your friend : in 2 player mode, you can shoot your partner and it will regenerate their shield. This comes in very handy!

Consejos

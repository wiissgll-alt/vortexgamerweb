Limited Edition Hang-On

Descripción

'limited edition hang-on' is a motorcycle-based racing game from sega and is a remix of their superb 1987 game, 'super hang-on'. Running on identical hardware, the graphics and gameplay remain the same, with players riding a turbo-powered bike trying to reach checkpoints within a tight time limit. As with 'super hang-on', players could press and hold a turbo button and push the bike to a breathtaking speed - necessary to beat the game's tight time limits. As with the original game, players can chose to race on any one of four courses: africa, asia, america and europe. Africa is the easiest and shortest of the four, with each subsequent course becoming both more demanding - with tighter bends and more aggressive rival racers - and having a greater number of stages to have to beat. Limited edition's only difference from 'super hang-on' is in its courses, which have been redesigned and are more forgiving than those of the original game.

L'Africa è la più semplice e la più breve delle quattro, e ogni percorso successivo diventa sempre più impegnativo - con curve più strette e rivali più aggressivi - e con un numero maggiore di tappe da battere. L'unica differenza tra l'edizione limitata e "super hang-on" è nei percorsi, che sono stati ridisegnati e sono più tolleranti di quelli del gioco originale.

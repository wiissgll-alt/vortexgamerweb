Descripción

The super abc-kit is a kit made by two-bits score was a multi-game kit that were to be connected to a pac-man or ms.pac-man board. The games on the kit are: pac-man, ms.pac-man, pac attack (a.k.a hangly man) ms.pac-attack (a.k.a ms.pac-man plus), ms.pac-man after dark (a.k.a vector ms.pac-man), and piranha (hack).

NAM-1975 (NGM-001)(NGH-001)

Descripción

One or two players take control of 2 machine-gun carrying vietnam vets who must shoot their way through a number of sideways scrolling, vietnam-themed levels. The players shoot into the screen and aiming is achieved via the use an on-screen target, which each player directs to lock on to the various enemy troops and vehicles. Players can run and somersault to avoid the incoming enemy fire. A number of more powerful weapons - such as grenades and flame throwers - are often dropped by the enemy and can be picked up and used. Bonus points are also revealed in this fashion. Nam-1975 is a blatant plagiarism of tad corp's "cabal", released in september 1988. Cabal is easily the more accomplished of the two games.

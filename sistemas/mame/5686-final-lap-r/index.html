Descripción

The last game in namco's seminal f1 series, which began eleven years' earlier with 1982's "pole position". Final lap r features the same loose handling and demanding game-play of its predecessors, but has the obligatory improved graphics, due to the more powerful host hardware. The game offers players a choice of four race tracks : 1. Germany 2. Brazil 3. Hungary 4. Belgium

Total Carnage (rev LA1 03/10/92)

Descripción

A pseudo-sequel to williams' superb 1990 shoot-em-up, "smash tv", total carnage is set in a futuristic parody of the first gulf war. General ahkboob, himself an obvious parody of sadam hussein, has set his vast army of infantry, tanks and biogenetic mutants onto the world. 1 or 2 players must fight through the enemy-packed levels, rescuing reporters and civilians as they go. Total carnage retains the dual-joystick control of its predecessor, but introduced new weapons, enemies and large, scrolling levels to the original game-play. As with "smash tv", total carnage features the infamous 'pleasuredome'; accessible only if players manage to collect the 220 keys that are hidden throughout the game's levels. Total carnage has a much higher difficulty level than that of "smash tv".

Descripción

Pirate release of space invaders. Items which make this game unique: * spaceship's design is different. * numbers in the bunker to denote rack number. * the occasional timed round, with a bonus plus a splash screen for finishing the rack. * invaders change shape as you finish off more racks. * when you finish off the first rack, the game asks you for your name.

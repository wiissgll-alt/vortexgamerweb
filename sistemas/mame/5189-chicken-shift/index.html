Chicken Shift

Descripción

An addictive puzzle game in which the object is to guide eggs through a maze of egg-runs and guide them safely to the basket at the bottom of the screen. This is achieved by switching red and blue pipes to face the left or right-hand side at exactly the right moment. Players must manipulate different pipes on both halves of the screen at the same time, making for demanding and rewarding gameplay.

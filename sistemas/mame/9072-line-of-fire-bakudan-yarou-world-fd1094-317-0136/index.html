Line of Fire / Bakudan Yarou (World, FD1094 317-0136)

Descripción

Line of fire is a lightgun controlled shoot-em-up for one or two players, in which two commandos find a special weapon - a vehicle mounted, automatic machine pistol - hidden deep within an enemy base. Before they can escape to report their findings, however, they find themselves surrounded by enemy forces and must blast their way through enemy lines and escape, using the enemies' own super weapon against them. As well as enemy troops, players will have to contend with artillery, tanks and helicopters. At the end of each stage they will face a powerful boss vehicle that must be destroyed to progress. Between stages, a map screen is displayed which shows the players' progress through the terrorist facility and towards freedom. Ammunition is limited to a few clips of bullets for the machine gun, a small supply of hand grenades and a screen clearing missile, although additional supplies can be obtained by shooting the relevant icons as they appear during play. Settings ranging from enemy bases to thick jungle.

Line of fire est un jeu de tir contrôlé par arme légère pour un ou deux joueurs, dans lequel deux commandos trouvent une arme spéciale - un pistolet-mitrailleur automatique monté sur véhicule - cachée au plus profond d'une base ennemie. Cependant, avant de pouvoir s'échapper pour rapporter leurs découvertes, ils se retrouvent encerclés par les forces ennemies et doivent se frayer un chemin à travers les lignes ennemies et s'échapper, en utilisant la super arme de l'ennemi contre eux. En plus des troupes ennemies, les joueurs devront affronter l'artillerie, les chars et les hélicoptères. Entre les étapes, un écran de carte s'affiche qui montre la progression des joueurs à travers l'installation terroriste et vers la liberté. Tra una fase e l'altra viene visualizzata una schermata della mappa che mostra i progressi dei giocatori attraverso la struttura terroristica e verso la libertà.

Balloon Brothers

Descripción

A puzzle game similar to "tetris" but with different colored balloons in different shapes. Instead of falling blocks, the balloons float to the top. Completing a full row across will make that row disappear.

A puzzle game similar to "tetris" but with different colored balloons in different shapes. Instead of falling blocks, the balloons float to the top. Completing a full row across will make that row disappear.

Un jeu de réflexion similaire à "Tetris" mais avec des ballons de différentes couleurs et formes différentes. Au lieu de blocs qui tombent, les ballons flottent vers le haut. Remplir une ligne complète fera disparaître cette ligne.

Un puzzle game simile al "tetris" ma con palloncini di colore diverso e di forme diverse. Invece di cadere, i palloncini fluttuano verso l'alto. Il completamento di un'intera riga farà scomparire quella riga.

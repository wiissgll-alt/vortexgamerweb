Descripción

You're invited as one of the master champions to engage in fighter's history! the world awaits for a legend to arrive, an ultimate honor for someone who is willing to risk it all in this contest. By mysterious invitation you are to join the greatest martial arts masters of the world. Only one person can walk away the ultimate champion by challenging 9 competitors and 2 bosses.

Continental Circus (US set 1)

Descripción

Continental circus is a formula one racing game in which the player races over a number of international grand prix circuits, trying to complete each lap before the time limit expires. The game features pit-stops and dynamic weather; the latter in the form of rain. If the player's car is hit by either a rival car or strikes a trackside barrier, the car will be damaged and begin to smoke. The player must 'pit' at the earliest opportunity to get the car repaired, otherwise the fire will spread and the car will eventually explode. Should the player's car have a second collision before the pit-stop is reached, the car to explode, wasting precious time before a replacement car appears. Continental Circus è un gioco di corse di formula 1 in cui il giocatore gareggia su una serie di circuiti di gran premi internazionali, cercando di completare ogni giro prima che scada il tempo limite.

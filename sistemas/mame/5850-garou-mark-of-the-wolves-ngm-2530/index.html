Descripción

The king of fighters tournament is not for the weak. Choose among 3 strong and powerful fighters - defeat all comers and take home the championship. As joe higashi, terry or andy bogard, it's your mission to defeat the evil and sinister geese howard - who holds all of southtown city in his grasp. Use your special skills and lightning fast reflexes to advance through the many stages of city streets. For only through fierce combat can you be crowned the king of fighters.

Consejos

  • Just Defend: Bloquea un ataque justo antes de que te golpee para recuperar una pequeña porción de salud.
  • Selección de color: En la pantalla de selección de personaje, mantén pulsado START y presiona un botón de ataque.
  • Just Defend: Block an attack just before it hits you to regain a small portion of health.
  • Color Select: At the character select screen, hold START and press an attack button.
  • Défendez simplement : bloquez une attaque juste avant qu’elle ne vous frappe pour retrouver une petite partie de votre santé.
  • Sélection de couleur : sur l'écran de sélection de personnage, maintenez START et appuyez sur un bouton d'attaque.
  • Difenditi e basta: blocca un attacco appena prima che ti colpisca per recuperare una piccola parte di salute.
  • Selezione colore: nella schermata di selezione del personaggio, tieni premuto START e premi un pulsante di attacco.
